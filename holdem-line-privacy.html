Privacy Policy – Hold'em Line

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Hold'em Line ("the game") is made by Qheyl Games, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

1. Information stored on your device

Your progress (missions, stars, Gold Bars, upgrades, commanders, Endless and Daily Challenge records, a battle in progress) and your settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. You choose when to watch an ad, for example for a second chance, a supply drop or double Gold Bars. Ads are limited to content rated PG. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • how you interact with ads and the app (for example app launches, taps and video views)
  • diagnostic information about app and ad performance

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

The game offers optional purchases with real money (the Supporter Pack, Gold Bar packs and commanders). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only which product was bought, so that it can be delivered and restored on a new install. Google Play's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not have user accounts or servers that store your data.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

On-device data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data collected by Google is handled under Google's policies linked above. For any request about your data, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

Data sent by the game is sent over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
